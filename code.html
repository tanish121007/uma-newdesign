Quality Control Protocol

Quality Is Built Into Every Box.

Our on-site testing facility ensures compliance with international TAPPI, ASTM, and FEFCO standards. Every production run undergoes automated batch sampling before client dispatch.

science Laboratory Certificate of Analysis

Every shipment is accompanied by a certified COA confirming tested GSM, Bursting Index, and Box Compression Test (BCT) values.

GSM Verification TAPPI T-410

Digital quadrantal scale verification ensuring exact paper liner and fluting medium grammage.

Mullen Burst Test ASTM D3786

Hydrostatic diaphragm burst testing to measure maximum pneumatic rupture resistance in kg/cm².

Edge Crush Test (ECT) TAPPI T-811

Direct compressive force measurement perpendicular to flutes, predicting vertical warehouse stackability.

Box Compression (BCT) ASTM D642

Full-scale hydraulic box crushing platform simulating multi-tier pallet storage under dynamic load.

Moisture Analysis 6.5% - 8.5% Target

Microprocessor moisture meters ensure paper fibers retain structural resilience and adhesive stability.

Caliper & Crease Gauge ±0.25mm Flange

Micrometer validation of overall board caliper and 90-degree folding crease memory during automated packaging.
